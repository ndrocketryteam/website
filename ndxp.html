NDXP

Notre Dame Experimental Propulsion: Pioneering the university's first liquid-propelled launch vehicle and a new frontier of aerospace engineering at Notre Dame.

Joining the Collegiate
Space Race

Fresh off the team's first USLI win in the 2023-24 cycle, we took a bold step forward, splitting our resources and personnel to begin an experimental propulsion arm of the team.

Notre Dame Experimental Propulsion (NDXP) represents the most technically ambitious project in the history of the university's aerospace programs. Our goal is singular: to launch Notre Dame's first liquid-propelled vehicle at a major competition by May 2029.

Target Milestones

The road to orbit requires precise engineering and relentless testing. Here is the operational timeline leading up to the 2029 competition.

MAY 2026

Engine Alpha
Hotfire

Initial static fire test of the first generation liquid propulsion system to validate plumbing, ignition, and thrust parameters.

MAY 2027

Engine Beta
Hotfire

Second iteration engine test. Incorporating regenerative cooling data, optimizing specific impulse, and ensuring flight-weight tolerances.

MAY 2027

First Vehicle
Design Finalized

Completion of CAD and structural simulations for the airframe, tanks, and avionics bays required to house the Beta engine.

MAY 2029

First Competition
Launch

Integration complete. Transport to the pad and ignition. Notre Dame's inaugural liquid launch at a major collegiate competition.
