Who We Are

The Team

Founded with the vision to bridge the gap between theoretical classroom learning and applied engineering, the Notre Dame Rocketry Team (NDRT) is a completely student-run organization.

Our members span multiple engineering disciplines—from Aerospace and Mechanical to Electrical and Computer Science. By designing, manufacturing, testing, and flying high-powered rocketry systems, we empower students to solve complex, real-world aerospace challenges.

Our Pillars

Core Goals

Engineering Education

To provide hands-on experience in the complete engineering lifecycle—from initial conceptual design and simulation to manufacturing and flight analysis.

Technical Innovation

To aggressively push limits by transitioning to advanced projects, such as internally developed flight computers, active drag systems, and liquid propulsion engines.

Community Outreach

To inspire the next generation of engineers by hosting STEM engagement events for local schools, proving that space exploration is accessible to everyone.

Our Legacy

Competition & Launch History

2011 – 2012

The Underdog Origins

Founded by Jim Lampariello and a crew of seven students in the basement of Fitzpatrick Hall. The team started from scratch, initially building rocket bodies out of simple phenolic resin before aggressively upgrading to aerospace-grade fiberglass and carbon fiber.

2011 – 2012

The Underdog Origins

2014

The Dream Team Catalyst

Under the new guidance of faculty advisor Aleksandar Jemcov, NDRT rapidly expanded its technical arsenal. The club transformed into an interdisciplinary powerhouse, recruiting top talent from electrical engineering, physics, and computer science to build complex custom payloads.

2014

The Dream Team Catalyst

2016 – 2018

Breaking Barriers

The era of high-powered certification and supersonic flight. The team standardized Level 2 and Level 3 rocketry certifications for its members, integrated advanced commercial avionics, and began pushing the boundaries of altitude and mach speeds, laying the technical foundation for future national titles.

2016 – 2018

Breaking Barriers

2019 – 2021

Pushing Boundaries

Engineered an autonomous Rover and UAV tag-team payload. The team proved they were a lethal competitive force, capturing the 3rd Place STEM Engagement Award and setting the stage for an unprecedented dominant streak.

2019 – 2021

Pushing Boundaries

2022

A Podium Finish

Celebrating their 10-year anniversary, NDRT unleashed a 12-foot behemoth named "Camulus", securing a massive 2nd Place Overall finish using a 360-degree Rotating Optical Imager (TROI) to locate their landing site without GPS.

2022

A Podium Finish

SPRING 2024

Undisputed National Champions

Total Domination: Out of 49 elite universities, the Fighting Irish were crowned 1st Place Overall, sweeping 1st in Safety, 1st in STEM, and 3rd in Reusable Launch Vehicle.

The gold-painted rocket "Dr. Joe" blasted to 5,200 ft. The payload featured a futuristic crew capsule that safely autorotated four "STEMnauts" back to earth—entirely without a parachute.

SPRING 2024

National Champions

FALL 2024

The Birth of NDXP

Riding the momentum of the national title, the team made its most ambitious pivot in history. Splitting resources and talent, they officially formed Notre Dame Experimental Propulsion (NDXP). The mission: transition away from solid commercial motors and begin the grueling R&D required to design, print, and hot-fire the university's first custom liquid bi-propellant rocket engine.

FALL 2024

The Birth of NDXP

2026

Cementing the Legacy

Tackled the "HAUS" challenge, building a habitat that safely landed four simulated astronauts and autonomously collected/tested soil samples. Secured 4th Place Overall and captured the 1st Place Safety Award once again.

2026

Cementing the Legacy
