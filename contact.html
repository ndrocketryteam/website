Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help direct your inquiry to the right place.

Are you looking to join the team?
We are always searching for passionate engineers, researchers, and innovators. If you want to help us build the future of aerospace, head over to our Join Us page to learn about our recruitment cycles, available positions, and how to apply.
Do you participate in STEM engagements or community outreach?
Absolutely. We love sharing our passion for rocketry with the community! From local school visits to regional science fairs, our team is dedicated to inspiring the next generation of engineers. Please visit our Outreach page to read more about our initiatives and to submit an event request.
Are you looking to sponsor the Notre Dame Rocketry Team?
Our sponsors make everything we do possible. Partnering with NDRT gives your organization access to top-tier engineering talent and unparalleled brand visibility on our vehicles and at nationwide competitions. Head over to our Sponsors page to view our sponsorship tiers, download our partnership packet, and learn how we can collaborate.
Can we send you physical mail or hardware?
Yes, however, please do not ship items directly without prior confirmation. Reach out to us using the contact form above first. We will coordinate a secure shipping address, handling instructions, and delivery windows with our logistics and lab safety team.
