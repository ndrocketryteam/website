About NDRT

At the Notre Dame Rocketry Team, we design, manufacture, test, and launch high-powered rockets. Our mission is to transform classroom theory into real-world engineering solutions.

By developing complex aerospace technologies like custom avionics and our new experimental liquid propulsion systems, our members gain hands-on experience that goes far beyond traditional coursework. Ultimately, our goal is to equip the next generation of engineers with practical skills, technical rigor, and a collaborative mindset.
