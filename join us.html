JOIN THE TEAM

Are you a student looking to get involved with our work? NDRT is entirely student-led, designing, building, and launching some of the most advanced undergraduate projects in the country.

Zero Barriers

We believe passion is the most important prerequisite. We require no application or dues to join the team. If you're willing to put in the work and learn, there is a place for you here.

All Majors Welcome

While we have many aerospace and mechanical engineers, we welcome students of all majors and backgrounds. We need software developers, business minds, media creators, and scientists.

Undergrad & Grad

Whether you are a first-year student looking to learn the ropes, or a graduate student bringing advanced fluid dynamics knowledge, our projects scale to challenge every experience level.

How to Join

1

Mid-Semester?
