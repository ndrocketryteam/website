interested in becoming a sponsor?

Building rockets is expensive. The Notre Dame Rocketry Team is heavily reliant on continued support from our sponsors to compete nationally, develop future engineers, and serve the Michiana community.

Our Mission Impact

Generous contributions from our sponsors allow us to inspire teamwork, creativity, and innovation within our members. NDRT values any amount of time, support, or financial contributions to help us continue our mission.

Why Partner With Us?

As a sponsor, you share heavily in our accomplishments. Sponsors benefit from massive social media and online exposure, active mentorship opportunities, and direct recruiting access to the university's most talented pool of aerospace engineers.

Our Supporters

Golden Dome Sponsors

Ansys, Inc.

Ansys is an industry leader in CFD simulation software that is used across the aerospace industry. The team will use Ansys' CFD software in the design of air frames for USLI, as well as studying fluid flow across the injector, chamber, and piping within Engine Alpha and future iterations for NDXP.
